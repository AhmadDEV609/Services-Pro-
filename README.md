ServicesPro – Service Booking & Management Platform
📌 Overview

ServicesPro is a service booking and management platform designed to connect customers with professional service providers. The platform allows service providers to showcase their services, enables customers to book appointments, and provides administrators with complete control over provider approvals and platform management.

The system includes three types of users: Admin, Provider, and Customer, each with specific roles and functionalities.

👥 User Roles & Functionalities
1. Admin

The Admin is responsible for managing and maintaining the platform.

Manage and monitor service providers.
Review provider profiles and submitted information.
Approve or reject provider registration requests.
Ensure that only approved providers are visible to customers.
Manage the overall platform and its activities.
2. Provider

Service Providers can register and create their professional profiles.

Register and log in to their accounts.
Create and manage their profiles.
Add and manage the services they offer.
Submit their profiles for admin approval.
Manage customer appointment bookings.

Important: A provider's profile and services will only become visible to customers after the Admin approves their profile.

3. Customer

Customers can explore available services and book appointments.

Register and log in to their accounts.
Browse approved service providers.
Explore available services.
View provider details and service information.
Book appointments with their preferred providers.
Select a preferred appointment date and time.
Choose a payment method.
Manage their bookings.
💳 Payment Methods

ServicesPro supports two payment methods to provide flexibility and convenience for customers.

1. Cash on Place
Customers can choose to pay in cash.
Payment is made directly at the service location when the provider delivers the service.
2. Online Payment
Customers can select online payment during the booking process.
Payments are handled through the integrated online payment system.
🔄 Platform Workflow

The application follows a structured workflow to ensure a reliable service booking experience.

User Registration: Providers and customers create their accounts.
Provider Profile Creation: Providers complete their profiles and add the services they offer.
Admin Approval: The Admin reviews provider profiles and approves or rejects them.
Service Visibility: Only approved providers and their services are displayed to customers.
Service Selection: Customers browse available providers and select their desired services.
Appointment Booking: Customers book appointments by selecting their preferred date and time.
Payment Selection: Customers choose between Cash on Place and Online Payment.
Service Delivery: Providers deliver the booked services to customers.
